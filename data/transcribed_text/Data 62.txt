Rate Decay:

$$\alpha = \frac{\alpha_0}{1 + \text{decay rate} \times \text{Epoch No.}}$$
$\alpha \rightarrow$ gradually reduce $\alpha$.
Why? At begin, $\alpha$ takes large steps. $\rightarrow$ First when training should be taken faster and when, the solution point come $\downarrow$ can made smaller $\alpha$, so that it can easily converge.
If $\alpha$ is constant $\longrightarrow$ [unclear] over shoot $\alpha$ smaller.
