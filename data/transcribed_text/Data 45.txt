Original Transformer Architecture:

Comprises of 2 blocks:
- Encoder (left) $\rightarrow$ Understands $i/p$ from $em/o/p$ along with context from $i/p$.
- Decoder (right) $\rightarrow$ o/p-only, targets $\rightarrow$ target seq.

This can operate in independent modes:
1. Encoder-Only: sentence-classification, Named Entity Recog.
2. Decoder-Only: for generation, Generative Text.
3. Seq-to-seq: Resp. Input and generative o/p.
  - Translation
  - Summarization

Transformer encoder/decoder tasks:

Two approaches:
1. Masked Language Modeling: Used by encoder models, like BERT.
  It randomly masks some $i/p$ and tries to predict original letters.
  (Refers to claim bidirectional context (looks at words both $B/A$ masked word)).

2. Causal Language Modeling: Used by decoder, models like GPT.
  Predicts the next token based on all previous tokens in seq.
