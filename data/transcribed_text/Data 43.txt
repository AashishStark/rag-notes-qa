# Integration & Management Services

* Amazon EC2 Auto Scaling
  * Dynamic scaling & scaling out based demand
  * Predictive scaling & scheduled actions out of predictive finality & ang based on demand.
* Auto Scaling Groups:
  * Collection of EC2 inst.
  * ① **Min. capacity**:
    * Min. no. of inst. after the launch of auto scale group.
  * ② **Desired capacity**:
    * Ideal no. of req. insts. to handle the current workload. (Def. of insts.)
    * Need to be very. (Def. of minimum)
  * ③ **Max. capacity**:
    * Upper limit of no. of insts.
    * Priority over scaling -> Scale UP/DOWN.
    * Based on traffic.

# Directing traffic with Elastic Load Balancing (ELB)
* To bal. traffic across a grp. of EC2 inst.
* ELB: Take req. and route them to inst.
* Manage traffic + upgrade
* **ESLT**: Designed to distribute w/s traffic to improve application scalability.
  * Can manage both internal & external traffic
  * To optimize performance / reliability.

# Managing & queuing (Event driven)
* **Tightly coupled arch.**
  * If a single component fails or overloads, it causes issues for other components or even the whole system.
* To overcome this:
  * Amazon Simple Queue Service (SQS)
  * Amazon Simple Notification Service (SNS)
