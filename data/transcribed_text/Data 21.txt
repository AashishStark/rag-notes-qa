### Printing arrays
- `reshape(4,3)` $\rightarrow$ 1st array
- `reshape(2,3,4)` $\rightarrow$ 3rd array
  $\lfloor \begin{matrix} 3 \times 4 \\ \text{ 2 arrays} \end{matrix}$
- `np.set_printoptions(threshold=sys.maxsize)`
  $\downarrow$
  to print everything instead of
  $[:] \quad [...]$
- `"*"` $\rightarrow$ Elementwise product
  - `a.sum (axis=0)` $\rightarrow$ sum of each column
  - `a.sum (axis = 1)` $\rightarrow$ sum of each row
  - `a.sum (axis = -1)` $\rightarrow$ sum of C.F. along each row

### Array functions
- `np.empty(9)`
- `np.ones((9)) $\rightarrow$ 1`
- `np.zeros((9,4))`

### Indexing, Slicing & Iterating
- `a[2]`
- `a[52:5]`
- `a[1:4:2] = 1000`
