Regularization :

already used loss :
$J(W,b) = \frac{1}{m} \sum_{i=1}^{m} L(\hat{y}^{(i)}, y^{(i)})$

with $Ry$ :
$l_2 \text{ Reg} : ||W||_2^2 = \sum_{j=1}^{n_x} w_j^2 = w^T w$
$\lambda \to \text{Reg} \cdot \text{parameter}$

$J(W^{(1)}, b^{(1)} ...) = \frac{1}{m} \sum_{i=1}^{m} L(\hat{y}^{(i)}, y^{(i)}) + \frac{\lambda}{2m} \sum_{l=1}^{L} ||W^{[l]}||_F^2$

$||W^{[l]}||_F^2 = \sum_{i=1}^{n^{[l]}} \sum_{j=1}^{n^{[l-1]}} (W_{ij}^{[l]})^2$

$\to \text{Frobenius Norm}$
