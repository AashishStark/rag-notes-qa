# Binary Classification:
- $o/p \rightarrow \begin{cases} 1 \rightarrow cat \\ 0 \rightarrow Not\, cat \end{cases}$
- $o/p \rightarrow Img$
- $I/P \rightarrow 3 \, RGB \rightarrow \downarrow\downarrow\downarrow 3 \, ch \text{ by } 4H$
- Image:
  - Input layer $\begin{bmatrix} r \\ g \\ b \end{bmatrix}$ (each $n_x$)
  - $X = \begin{bmatrix} | & | & & | \\ x^{(1)} & x^{(2)} & \dots & x^{(m)} \\ | & | & & | \end{bmatrix}_{n_x \times m}$
  - $X\text{-shape } = (n_x, m)$

## Notations:

# Logistic:
- Input $= x$, $o/p = \hat{y} = P(y=1|x)$
- Parameters: $w, b$, $w \in \mathbb{R}^{n_x}, b \in \mathbb{R}$
- Output: $\hat{y} = \sigma(w^Tx + b)$
- $\sigma(z) = \frac{1}{1 + e^{-z}}$
- $z$ is too small $\Rightarrow \sigma(z) = 0$
- $z$ is too large $\Rightarrow \sigma(z) = 1$

# Loss/Cost fun:
- $L(\hat{y}, y) = -(y \log(\hat{y}) + (1-y)\log(1-\hat{y}))$
- $y = 0 \rightarrow z = -(1-\hat{y}) \log(1-\hat{y}) + [unclear]$
- $y = 1 \rightarrow z = -y \log(\hat{y}) \rightarrow$ big

- $J(w, b) = \frac{1}{m} \sum_{i=1}^{m} L(\hat{y}^{(i)}, y^{(i)})$

# GD:
- same as logis.
- $w = w - \alpha \frac{\partial J(w, b)}{\partial w}$
- $\alpha = \text{learning rate}$
- $J = 1 - \epsilon = \frac{d(J(w, b))}{d \hat{y}}$
